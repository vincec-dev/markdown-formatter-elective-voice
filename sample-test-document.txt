PRE-APPOINTMENT INSTRUCTIONS FOR DENTAL IMPLANT SURGERY:

Please follow these instructions carefully before your scheduled implant procedure:

48 Hours Before:
- Avoid alcohol and smoking
- Continue taking your regular medications unless otherwise instructed
- If you've been prescribed antibiotics, start taking them as directed
- Arrange for someone to drive you home after the procedure

Day Before:
- Eat a good meal the night before (you'll need to fast for 8 hours before surgery if sedation is used)
- Get a good night's sleep
- Do NOT take aspirin or ibuprofen (these can increase bleeding)

Day of Surgery:
- Brush and floss thoroughly
- Do NOT eat or drink anything for 8 hours before your appointment if you're receiving IV sedation
- Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing
- Leave jewelry and valuables at home
- Bring your insurance card and photo ID
- Arrive 15 minutes early to complete paperwork

POST-PROCEDURE CARE AFTER DENTAL IMPLANT:

Immediate Care (First 24 Hours):
- Bite down on gauze for 30-45 minutes to control bleeding
- Apply ice packs to face (20 minutes on, 20 minutes off) to reduce swelling
- DO NOT rinse, spit, or use straws (this can dislodge the blood clot)
- Take prescribed pain medication as directed
- Stick to soft, cold foods (ice cream, yogurt, smoothies)
- Sleep with your head elevated

First Week:
- Continue ice therapy for first 48 hours
- After 24 hours, gently rinse with warm salt water (1/2 teaspoon salt in 8 oz water) 3-4 times daily
- Avoid hard, crunchy, or spicy foods
- Do not smoke or use tobacco products
- Take all prescribed medications
- Gentle brushing is okay, but avoid the surgical site

Signs to Call Us Immediately:
- Excessive bleeding that doesn't stop
- Severe pain not controlled by medication
- Fever over 101°F
- Significant swelling after 3 days
- Numbness or tingling that persists

Q: How long does a dental implant procedure take?
A: The surgical placement of the implant typically takes 1-2 hours. However, the complete process from start to finish (including healing time) usually takes 3-6 months.

Q: Is the implant procedure painful?
A: You'll be numb during the procedure, so you won't feel pain. Some discomfort after surgery is normal and can be managed with prescribed pain medication. Most patients report that the procedure was easier than they expected!!!

Q: How do I care for my dental implants?
A: Care for them just like natural teeth!!! Brush twice daily, floss once daily, and visit us for regular checkups every 6 months. With proper care, implants can last a lifetime!!!
